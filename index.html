ISSB Ready

A study companion for candidates preparing for the Inter Services Selection Board (ISSB): psychology test practice, timed MCQ tests, a full mock exam and condensed study notes. Core content works offline.

Get it on Google Play

What's inside

  • Psychology practice: WAT, SCT, SRT, TAT, SDT and pointer stories
  • Timed MCQs: verbal and non-verbal intelligence, general knowledge, Pakistan affairs, English, maths and mechanical aptitude
  • Interview, group discussion and GTO preparation notes
  • ISSB date countdown, streaks and progress stats
Not a government app. ISSB Ready is an independent, private study app made by an individual developer. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to the ISSB, the Pakistan Armed Forces or the Government of Pakistan. For official information, visit issb.gov.pk.
